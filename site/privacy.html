Spectral Camera

Privacy policy

Effective date: 20 August 2026

Summary

Spectral Camera is an offline-first Android camera. The core app does not require an account, does not include advertising or analytics, and does not upload camera frames or photos to a developer-operated server.

Camera

The app requests camera access to display the live preview and capture photographs. Camera frames are processed on the device for the selected photographic rendering. The app does not transmit those frames to the developer or a remote processing service.

Photos and media

When you capture an image, Spectral Camera saves the result to the device through Android's media system. The app may request photo-library access when you choose to recover previous captures or import an image. That access is user-directed and remains on the device unless you explicitly use Android's sharing controls.

Data collection and sharing

Spectral Camera does not collect or share personal data through a developer-operated network service. The app does not use a login, advertising identifier, analytics SDK, cloud photo backup, or remote image-processing endpoint in the released product.

Android itself may provide normal platform services such as permission management, device backups, or system sharing. Those services are controlled by Android and the choices you make on your device.

Children

The app is not designed as a service for children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If the app's data behavior changes, this policy will be updated before or with the relevant release. The effective date above identifies the current version of this policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Spectral Camera publisher through the support contact shown on the official Google Play listing.
