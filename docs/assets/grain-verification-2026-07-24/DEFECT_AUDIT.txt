=== DEFECT AUDIT 2: chroma grain feature size vs luma ===
  spectral centroid (0=coarse, 1=fine):
    luma grain            = 0.2310
    chroma SHIPPED (x1.7) = 0.3570  ratio vs luma = 1.54x
    chroma FIXED   (/1.7) = 0.1288  ratio vs luma = 0.56x
  >> shipped chroma noise is FINER than luma noise. Real colour film dye clouds are
     COARSER than silver grains, and human chroma acuity is ~3x lower than luma.
     Fine high-frequency chroma noise is exactly what reads as colour fringing /
     chromatic aberration rather than film speckle.
